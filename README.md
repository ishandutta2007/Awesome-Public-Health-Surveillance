# Awesome-Public-Health-Surveillance

## Top Public Health Surveillance Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Disease Surveillance, Outbreak Response, Contact Tracing, Case Management & Health Data Systems*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Public Health Surveillance**. These systems support indicator- and event-based surveillance, outbreak detection and response, contact tracing, case management, and national or regional health data reporting.



**Examples** include AIMS Platform, SORMAS, DHIS2, CommCare, Go.Data, OpenMRS, REDCap, ArcGIS Survey123, Epi Info, and BlueDot (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Public health surveillance has one of the strongest open-source ecosystems of any domain. **SORMAS**, **DHIS2**, **Go.Data**, **OpenMRS**, **Epi Info**, and related tools are widely deployed by ministries of health and international organizations. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[AIMS Platform](https://www.aimsplatform.com/)** (or similar commercial surveillance offerings)  

  Commercial or specialized platforms used for advanced surveillance analytics and situational awareness in some jurisdictions.



- **[SORMAS](https://www.sormas.org/)**  

  Open-source surveillance and outbreak response system that also has organizational support and deployment services; listed here for its dual open/commercial ecosystem presence.



- **[DHIS2](https://www.dhis2.org/)**  

  Dominant open-source health information platform used by many countries as a national system; commercial hosting and implementation partners are widely available.



- **[CommCare](https://www.dimagi.com/commcare/)**  

  Mobile-first data collection and case management platform widely used in public health programs, with strong offline capabilities and commercial support options.



- **[Go.Data](https://www.who.int/tools/godata)**  

  WHO-supported outbreak investigation and contact-tracing tool that is freely available and open-source oriented.



- **[OpenMRS](https://openmrs.org/)**  

  Leading open-source electronic medical record platform often integrated into broader surveillance and clinical data flows; commercial support ecosystems exist.



- **[REDCap](https://projectredcap.org/)**  

  Secure web application for building and managing online surveys and databases, extensively used in research and public health data collection.



- **[ArcGIS Survey123](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-survey123/overview)**  

  Esri’s form and field data collection tool frequently used for public health surveys and geospatial surveillance workflows.



- **[Epi Info](https://www.cdc.gov/epiinfo/index.html)**  

  CDC’s public-domain suite for epidemiologic data collection, analysis, and visualization (widely used and freely available).



- **[BlueDot](https://bluedot.global/)**  

  Commercial infectious disease intelligence platform focused on early warning, risk assessment, and global outbreak monitoring.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[SORMAS](https://github.com/SORMAS-Foundation/SORMAS-Project)**  

  Surveillance Outbreak Response Management and Analysis System — open-source platform for epidemic and pandemic preparedness, case and contact management, and outbreak response.



- **[DHIS2](https://github.com/dhis2)**  

  District Health Information Software 2 — the most widely adopted open-source health management information system for national and sub-national data collection, analysis, and reporting.



- **[Go.Data](https://github.com/WorldHealthOrganization/godata)**  

  WHO open-source outbreak investigation tool supporting case management, contact tracing, and visualization of chains of transmission.



- **[OpenMRS](https://github.com/openmrs)**  

  Open-source electronic medical record system used globally; frequently integrated with surveillance and public health reporting workflows.



- **[Epi Info](https://www.cdc.gov/epiinfo/)**  

  CDC’s free public-domain software suite for epidemiologic data entry, statistical analysis, mapping, and graphing.



- **[REDCap](https://projectredcap.org/)**  

  Secure, web-based application for building and managing online surveys and databases (widely used in public health and research; source available under certain conditions).



- **[CommCare (and related open components)](https://www.dimagi.com/commcare/)**  

  Mobile case management platform with strong offline support; core technology and community editions support open deployment models.



- **[SORMAS–DHIS2 interoperability projects](https://github.com/SORMAS-Foundation)**  

  Open connectors and adapters enabling data exchange between SORMAS and DHIS2.



- **[Additional outbreak and contact-tracing open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community and academic projects for contact follow-up, transmission visualization, and field data collection.



- **[Geospatial and survey open frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Open tools (including ODK/KoboToolbox ecosystems) frequently used for public health field surveys and mapping.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **DHIS2** as the national or organizational HMIS backbone.

- Using **SORMAS** for dedicated outbreak detection and response.

- Implementing **Go.Data** for contact tracing and chain-of-transmission analysis during outbreaks.

- Combining **OpenMRS** clinical data with surveillance platforms for integrated case detection.

- Leveraging **Epi Info** and **REDCap** for rapid epidemiologic investigations and research databases.

- Accepting that commercial intelligence platforms (BlueDot and similar) add value for global early-warning analytics and proprietary data feeds.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Adopt DHIS2 for routine surveillance and reporting → deploy SORMAS or Go.Data for outbreak response and contact tracing → integrate clinical data via OpenMRS where appropriate → use Epi Info/REDCap for ad-hoc investigations → visualize with open geospatial tools. This stack is fully open, production-proven, and used by ministries of health worldwide. Commercial platforms remain useful for specialized analytics or when organizations prefer fully managed services.
